# SEAGARA - Coastal Cold Chain & Utility System Dashboard

> *Solar-powered coastal utility hub providing clean water and food-grade ice slurry for coastal communities and fishermen, powered by an integrated AI resource-management layer.*

---

## 📌 Overview
**SEAGARA** (Seanergy Raya) is an integrated conceptual engineering prototype designed to support coastal areas through sustainable energy management, seawater water-treatment systems, and efficient cold-chain infrastructure (ice slurry production). 

This repository contains the dashboard and documentation for the SEAGARA project, developed as part of the Pertamuda business and innovation challenge.

---

## ⚙️ Core Services
1. **Clean Water Supply:** Reliable and accessible clean water production for coastal households.
2. **Ice Slurry Cold Chain:** Food-grade ice slurry (~0°C) production to optimize fish preservation, reduce quality loss, and enhance fishermen's net income.
3. **Solar-First Energy Strategy:** Powered by a baseline 500 Wp Solar PV setup, stabilized with a LiFePO4 battery storage system (24V 100Ah).
4. **AI Resource Management:** Predictive analysis, energy-demand forecasting, and production scheduling layer.

---

## 📊 Technical Architecture & Baseline
* **Solar PV:** 500 Wp baseline rooftop solar setup
* **Energy Storage:** 2.4 kWh LiFePO4 Battery (24V, 100 Ah)
* **Refrigeration & Slurry:** ~0°C ice slurry target, 30 kg/day baseline production
* **Hydraulic System:** Seawater intake & loop running at ~10 L/min
